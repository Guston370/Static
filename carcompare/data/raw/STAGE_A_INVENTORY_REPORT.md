## 3. Disambiguation Rules: Variants vs Models

1. **Special / Cosmetic Editions are NOT separate models**:
   - *Tata Dark Edition, Red Dark, Kaziranga, Camo* are aesthetic variant packages under Nexon, Harrier, Safari.
   - *Skoda Monte Carlo* is an equipment trim under Kushaq and Slavia.
2. **Sport / Performance Trim Lines are Variants**:
   - *Hyundai N Line (i20 N Line, Venue N Line, Creta N Line)* are performance variants with suspension/exhaust tuning, not distinct models.
   - *Tata Altroz Racer* is a hot-hatch trim of Altroz.
   - *Volkswagen GT / GT Plus* are engine/transmission variants of Virtus and Taigun.
3. **Alternative Powertrains are Variants**:
   - Dual-fuel CNG trims (*Maruti S-CNG, Tata iCNG, Hyundai Hy-CNG, Toyota Hy-CNG*) are powertrain variants within the respective model lines.
   - Strong hybrid options (*Toyota Urban Cruiser Hyryder Strong Hybrid, Maruti Grand Vitara Strong Hybrid, Honda City e:HEV*) are powertrain variants.
4. **Dedicated EV Sub-Brands vs Platform Lines**:
   - *TATA.ev*: Cataloged as distinct brand entities (*Tiago.ev, Tigor.ev, Punch.ev, Nexon.ev, Curvv.ev*) due to dedicated *acti.ev* pure-electric platforms and dedicated retail stores.
   - *Mahindra*: *XUV400* is cataloged as an electric model; upcoming *BE 6e* and *XEV 9e* are cataloged as INGLO-born models.
