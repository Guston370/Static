## 6. Audit & Review Protocol for Future Admissions

To transition any model from this Unresolved Report into `india-bike-models.json`:
1. **Manufacturer Confirmation**: Must have an active specification page on the manufacturer's official `.in` or India-specific website.
2. **Pricing Transparency**: Must have a published ex-showroom price applicable to at least one major Indian metro (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, or Chennai).
3. **VAHAN / ARAI Evidence**: Must be homologated for legal Indian road registration.
4. **Hardware Variant Detail**: Must provide distinct specifications for braking, wheels, and kerb weight.
